Our Understanding of Your Business

Live sport produces more data than any commentary team can use, and new fans need help to follow what is happening. TryZone IQ uses generative AI to turn live Opta data into short, plain-language insights, with analysts approving what is published. It was built by Capgemini with Stats Perform and AWS, debuted at the Women's Rugby World Cup 2025 as the first use of generative AI at any Rugby World Cup, and has since run at the Guinness Six Nations and the Quilter Nations Series.1, 2

We'd like to confirm this with you.
TryZone IQ · The offer

A generative AI insight platform for live data. It takes historical and live Opta match data, uses generative AI models hosted on Amazon Bedrock to draft short insights on player impact, team trends and standout performances, and passes them to analysts who review and approve them before they are published.2 Approved insights flow into the live broadcast production workflow for commentators and on-screen graphics, and out to digital match centres and social channels.2 It now also produces insights in French, Italian and Welsh as well as English.2
